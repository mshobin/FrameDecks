## FrameDecks v26.11.5

Controls that float. The Command Center and the phone remote now show their buttons straight on the glass, with no
box around each one: calmer to look at, and what matters during a meeting stands out on its own. Download from
**framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all
your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Floating controls in the Command Center** (Alt+Space): each chip opens on one frosted glass sheet, and nothing
  inside it is boxed any more. Buttons sit on the glass and show a soft fill only when you point at them or press
  them. Colour is kept for what it means: Go Live glows green when you are on air, Next Frame stays gold, Hide all
  stays red, a tool that is on is tinted. Groups are separated by space and one thin line instead of frames.

## Enhancements
- **The phone remote has the same floating look**: its buttons, tools and sections sit straight on the page with a
  soft fill while pressed. Text boxes, the top bar, the tabs and Memory Card messages keep their edges.
- Readable over anything: the glass sheet stays behind the Command Center, so the controls read the same over a
  dark Studio, a white document or a busy video call, in the light and dark themes.
