## FrameDecks v26.11.34

Better with Bluetooth microphones. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It
installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and
recordings. Listening already worked with any microphone Windows has, Bluetooth too; this release makes it easier to
choose one and keeps listening going when one drops out.

## What's New
- **Choose the microphone.** **Settings → How people see it → Listening → Microphone**: Listen for scripture, Voice
  commands and the Teleprompter's Follow my voice all listen with the one you choose here (the same choice as the
  Teleprompter's list). A Bluetooth headset's call microphone is marked as one.
- **Listening goes on if the microphone drops out.** If the chosen microphone stops during a meeting (a Bluetooth
  headset switched off or out of range), FrameDecks goes on listening with the Windows default microphone and tells you
  once. The next meeting tries your chosen microphone again.
- **The Ready check knows Bluetooth.** It warns when the chosen microphone is a Bluetooth headset's call microphone
  (its sound is thin, so listening may miss words), or when the chosen microphone is not connected, with **Choose a
  microphone**.

## Enhancements
- A USB microphone, or a wireless one with its own receiver, is suggested where it would hear better.

## Fixes
- Polls, name tags and members' phones never open over the Welcome on a new install: they wait until it is
  finished or skipped.

## Performance Improvements
None in this version.

## Known Issues
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
