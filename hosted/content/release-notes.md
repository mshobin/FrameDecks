## FrameDecks v26.11.25

What needs you now reaches you during the meeting, even with the Studio behind Zoom or Teams. Download from
**framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your
meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **The corner card.** While the Studio is minimised or behind Zoom or Teams, a card in the corner of the screen shows
  what waits for you, large enough to read at a glance:
  - **A verse heard** (said by you, or by a member in the meeting): the reference in large type, with **Show** and
    **Dismiss**.
  - **Prayer requests**: the newest one to read, with **Show** (no name), **Show with the name**, **Done**, **Later**
    (until another arrives) and **All requests** (opens the Studio's Members' phones).
  It sits on top of the meeting without taking your keyboard, is never part of screen sharing (even sharing the whole
  screen), and goes away by itself when nothing waits. With Sounds on, a soft chime tells you something new arrived.
  Settings → General → Corner card (on).
