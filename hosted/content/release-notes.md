## FrameDecks v26.11.9

The camera keeps up. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over
every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **The camera keeps up with your meeting app by itself.** If background blur makes FrameDecks vCam send fewer
  frames than Zoom or Teams asks for, FrameDecks lightens the blur in two steps (it finds your outline half as often,
  then uses the lighter classic blur) and goes back to your setting as soon as the PC has room again. The support
  report says when it has done this.

## Performance Improvements
- **Background blur is about a quarter to a third faster** on every PC, with the same picture: the blurred
  background and you are now blended in whole numbers.
- A very light blur is no longer worked out on the full-size picture, which on an older PC took longer than a whole
  frame; half size looks the same.

## Known Issues
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
