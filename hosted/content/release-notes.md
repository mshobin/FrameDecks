## FrameDecks v26.11.48
Delete a whole meeting in one go, every new meeting starts with a blank Frame, and members' phones fill the phone, with
sharp slides to zoom into and look back at.
Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and
keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Delete a meeting.** The bin button beside the meeting's name (in the Studio and on the website's Plan page) deletes
  that meeting with all its Frames, Decks and Content after asking. Other meetings are not touched. In the Studio,
  **Undo** brings it back. The meeting on screen cannot be deleted on the website while it is live.
- **A new meeting starts with a blank Frame**, ready for **+ Content**, in the Studio and on the website.
- **Members' phones fill the phone.** The FrameDecks name at the top, your meeting's name below it, what is on screen
  in the middle and **On screen · Prayer · Notes** at the bottom, within reach of a thumb. It uses the whole screen,
  around the notch and the home bar.
- **Zoom into a slide.** Tap the slide on a member's phone: it opens full screen with a sharp picture. Pinch or
  double-tap to zoom, drag to look around.
- **Look back at slides.** In the full-screen slide, swipe (or ◀ ▶) back through the slides already shown in this
  meeting. Slides still to come are never on phones. **Now** comes back to the slide on screen.
- **Slides follow the animations.** With a deck PowerPoint plays, phones get the slide as people see it, with the
  animations played so far.
- **Keep slides off phones.** In Slides → **Run order**, **📱 On phones / Off phones** keeps one slide off members'
  phones; Content Settings → **On members' phones** keeps a whole Deck off them. Phones then read *This slide is not
  shown on phones*, without its picture or words.
- **Slides in the sermon notes.** When you share the sermon notes, **Include the slides shown** adds the slides shown
  while members' phones were open at the end of the notes, with their pictures, so members keep them and can save
  them as a PDF.

## Enhancements
- The meeting's name on members' phones is larger and sits under the FrameDecks name.

## Fixes
- **Members' phones kept following with a slide on screen.** Since 26.11.45 the slide's picture could make what the PC
  sends larger than the workspace accepts (16,000 bytes), so phones could stop following the meeting while some slides
  were on screen. The small picture now always fits, and the sharp one goes separately.

## Performance Improvements
- Each sharp slide is sent once per slide (and again only after an animation), and only while members' phones is open.

## Known Issues
- The sharp slides, looking back and slides in the notes arrive with an update to the FrameDecks website. Until then,
  phones keep the small picture of the slide on screen and everything else works as before.
- Slides are kept for the meeting only: they go when members' phones closes.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
