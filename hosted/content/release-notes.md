## FrameDecks v26.11.60
Memory Cards are now called Chat Bubbles, and pasting a chat on your phone is much easier.
Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and
keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Chat Bubbles and recordings.

## What's New
- **Chat Bubbles.** Memory Cards are now called Chat Bubbles everywhere: in the Studio, the Command Center, Settings,
  the phone remote, Help and the website. They work exactly as before, and your pinned ones are kept.
- **Room to paste on your phone.** Tap the box under Engage › Chat Bubbles and the picture, Previous / Next and the
  tabs step aside, so the box sits at the top of the screen above the keyboard and the phone's own Paste bubble is in
  plain sight. The box is also taller. Tap **Done** to bring everything back.

## Enhancements
- The phone remote always shows a **Paste** button. Where the phone lets FrameDecks read what you copied, it pastes
  straight in; otherwise it opens the box and reminds you to hold inside it and tap Paste.
- Searching the Command Center for "memory" still finds Chat Bubbles.

## Fixes
- On the phone remote, the keyboard no longer covers the box you paste a chat into.

## Performance Improvements
- No changes in this release.

## Known Issues
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Check the download's SHA-256 on the download page first. Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is
  published.
