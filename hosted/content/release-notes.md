## FrameDecks v26.11.28

Prepared before, confident during. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It
installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and
recordings.

## What's New
- **Ready check.** Under **Go Live** in the Studio, and above it in the Command Center: one card that looks at the
  camera, your light and framing, the microphone, your meeting app (Zoom, Teams or Meet), this meeting's scriptures and
  media, and the PC (plugged in, battery saver, memory). Each line says what it found, with its one fix where there is
  one, like **Fetch the words** for a scripture that has none yet. It never stops you going live.
- **Light and framing hints.** While the ready check is open, FrameDecks looks at your camera picture and suggests,
  gently: "Your face is in shadow. Turn on a lamp in front of you", "You're near the right edge of the picture", "Sit
  back a little". Nothing changes by itself.
- **Next week, in one click.** **Next week** beside the meeting name makes next week's meeting: the same Frames, Decks,
  looks and timings, dated a week later, with the scripture and questions left empty to fill in. Undo is there.
- **The Moment.** The meeting summary has a third view: one picture to share, in your meeting's colours, with the verse,
  a question to carry into the week and when you meet next. **Save the picture**, **Copy the picture**, **WhatsApp**, or
  **Share with members' phones**.
- **Seasons, Christmas too.** Settings → Meeting & Branding → **Season**: Advent, Christmas, Easter or Harvest. One
  choice colours every card, on the camera and the shared screen, with a small touch of the season: a star and a gentle
  snowfall at Christmas. **No season** puts your own colours back.
- **Voice search.** With the Command Center's search open, hold **Right Ctrl** (or the microphone) and say what you are
  looking for: it is typed into the search, and you choose. Nothing runs by itself. It works when nothing is live too.

## Performance Improvements
- The camera's light and framing are looked at only while the ready check is open, a few times a second at most.

## Known Issues
- Light and framing need FrameDecks vCam and a face the camera can see; the microphone and meeting app are checked in
  the FrameDecks app on Windows.
- The snowfall shows on the shared screen; on the camera, Christmas brings its colours and gold.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
