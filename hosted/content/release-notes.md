## FrameDecks v26.11.3

The Command Center floats on your desktop with nothing behind it: only the glass bar, its chips and the panel a chip
opens. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier
version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **A new front page on framedecks.com**: a meeting builds itself on arrival and flies into the real Studio, then
  each part of the program (the Command Center, the Teleprompter, Spotlight, Control and the phone remote) shows
  itself as you scroll, with the keys the program really uses. The public website is always dark; Light, Dark and
  Auto are in your workspace after you sign in.

## Enhancements
- The Help pages and the guide show pictures of the current program.

## Fixes
- **The Command Center no longer shows a black box behind its glass.** With FrameDecks in the tray (or any time it
  opened over the desktop), the window behind the bar and its chips stayed black instead of see-through. It was
  easy to miss over the dark Studio and plain to see over a light desktop.

## Performance Improvements
None in this version.

## Known Issues
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
