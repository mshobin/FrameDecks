## FrameDecks v26.11.49
Meetings are easier to find and start, and running PowerPoint slides is simpler: one Present button, a Next that says
what it will do, and your phone as a clicker.
Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and
keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Meeting tabs.** With more than one meeting, the meetings are tabs above the Frames. Each tab shows the date and
  how many Frames it has, the next meeting by date says *Next*, and past ones are quieter. One click switches.
- **"This PC runs".** A line under the tabs says which meeting this PC will run, with its date, time and Frames.
- **New meeting from…** New offers **Blank** (one empty Frame) or **Copy of** the meeting shown: the same Frames,
  Decks and looks, with the scripture and questions left empty to fill in. The website offers the same choice.
- **Up next on the website.** On the Plan page, the next meeting by date is marked *Up next*, and past meetings are
  quieter.
- **Present slides.** With a Frame that has slides selected, the big button reads **Present slides**. It goes live
  and shows the slide large, with big **Back** and **Next** buttons, the next slide, its notes, a timer and a strip of
  the slides in this meeting's order.
- **Next says what it will do.** While slides are on screen, the big button reads **Next animation** or **Next
  slide**. After the last slide it goes to the next Frame and says so.
- **Your phone as a clicker.** On the phone remote, **Clicker** under *Slide 3 of 12* shows the slide, two big
  buttons and what Next will do.
- **Ready when you add it.** A deck's Content Settings say at once how it will play, for example *Ready: 24 slides,
  3 with animations · PowerPoint will play it*, or *still slides (PowerPoint is not on this PC)*. The small pictures
  are made straight away, so the slides grid and phones are quick when it goes live.

## Enhancements
- **Capture Memory is now Capture Chats**, and it no longer sits below Decks in the Frames column. Open it from
  **Engage → Memory Cards**; **Engage → Capture copies**, the Command Center, the tray and the phone remote work as
  before.
- The slides window has fewer buttons: **Previous**, **Next**, **Zoom** and **Present**. Run order, the hold
  screens, where you are and members' phones are under **More ▾**.
- The line under the deck's name says how it plays in plain words: *PowerPoint is playing this deck*, *Still slides
  (no animations)*, *Slide 4 is not on members' phones*.

## Fixes
- None in this release.

## Performance Improvements
- A deck's small pictures are made in the background when it is added, not the first time the slides grid opens.

## Known Issues
- Animations still need Windows with PowerPoint installed; otherwise the slides play as still pictures, and the deck's
  Content Settings say why.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
