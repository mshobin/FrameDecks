## FrameDecks v26.11

A big update: the Teleprompter can now follow your voice, and FrameDecks has a new kind of version number. From now
on a version is the year and the month it came out, so 26.11 is November 2026. Download from
**framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all
your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Follow my voice**: in the Teleprompter, choose **Follow my voice** (or press **V**) and read. The script moves
  as you speak: the words you have read go quieter and the next ones stay at the reading line. Pause, slow down or
  talk off script, and it waits for you.
- **It listens only while the Teleprompter is pinned** above every other window. Unpin, hide or close it and the
  microphone is let go straight away.
- **New version numbers**: the year and the month of the release (26.11). A second release in the same month adds
  a number (26.11.1).

## Enhancements
- Choose the microphone and the sensitivity beside **Follow my voice**; the microphone you use in Zoom or Teams works
  for both at once.
- Scroll by hand at any time and the voice carries on from there. **▶ Scroll** and **Follow my voice** take turns.
- The help pages read as one current guide, without the version numbers of earlier releases.

## Fixes
- The Studio no longer gets heavier the longer you work: each Frame you chose used to leave its preview behind in
  memory, and every one kept redrawing. After a long session of planning it now uses about 4 MB instead of over
  100 MB, and stays quick on older PCs.

## Performance Improvements
- Follow my voice is made for older PCs with 4 GB of memory: it runs only while it is on, in the background at low
  priority, listens only for the words of your script and skips silence. It needs about 350 MB of free memory and
  says so when the PC has less.
- The voice files (about 53 MB) download only the first time you turn it on, and can be removed again from the
  Teleprompter. Nothing you say is recorded, kept or sent anywhere.

## Known Issues
- Follow my voice understands English scripts. Names and unusual words are skipped; the words around them keep it on
  track.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
