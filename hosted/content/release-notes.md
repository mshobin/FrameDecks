## FrameDecks v26.11.24

Bring everyone's phone into the meeting, give members the sermon notes, and let a verse said by a member reach the
screen with one tap. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every
earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Members' phones.** Studio → Engage → **Members' phones** → Open. One code and one QR for everyone in the meeting,
  with no account: on framedecks.com/follow their phone shows what is on the meeting screen as it changes (the Frame,
  the question, the scripture, in large readable type), and takes prayer requests when you switch them on. **Show the
  code on screen** puts it on the picture for people on Zoom or Teams. Only what is already on the meeting screen is
  shared; never your Teleprompter script or anything not shown.
- **Sermon notes.** When a meeting ends, its summary has a second view: **Sermon notes**, made from what was presented:
  each Frame's Teleprompter script (what you planned to say; you can leave it out), every scripture read, in full, and
  the questions to reflect on. Nothing to fill in. **Print / Save as PDF**, **Copy**, **Email**, or **Share with members'
  phones**, where it appears as a calm reading page they can save as a PDF.
- **Scripture said by members.** Settings → Scripture → Listen for scripture → **Members**: FrameDecks also hears what
  this PC plays (the others on Zoom or Teams), for Bible references only. When a member says "John three sixteen",
  you are offered it, marked "Heard in the meeting", and you choose Show or Dismiss. It is never shown by Auto.
- **Meeting summary** (first in this version): when a meeting ends, each Frame and how long it was on against the plan,
  the questions and the scriptures, to print, save as PDF, copy or email.
- **Prayer requests** (first in this version): people send a request from their phone; you read each one first and
  show it anonymously or with the name they gave; **We're praying for…** gathers the ones shown.

## Enhancements
- **Private by design.** Phones get only what is on the meeting screen (not birthday greetings, which name a member).
  Prayer requests are deleted from the cloud the moment this PC takes them in, and kept on this PC only until you close
  Members' phones. The meeting's sound is heard on this PC only, for Bible references, and never recorded.
- **Light on phones.** A phone asks for changes every few seconds only while the page is on screen, and gets a tiny
  answer when nothing changed.
- **Smart Next starts again** from what a Frame shows when it is taken again.

## Fixes
- Smart Next could skip straight to the next Frame after a Frame was taken a second time.

## Performance Improvements
None in this version.

## Known Issues
- Members' phones and prayer requests need this PC connected to your FrameDecks workspace (Settings → Sync).
- Hearing the meeting needs the FrameDecks app on Windows and works with the PC's default speakers or headset.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
