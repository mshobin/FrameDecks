## FrameDecks v26.11.22

FrameDecks feels quicker, calmer and more forgiving. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks
update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned
Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Undo for 5 seconds.** Next, Previous, Hide all, Black screen and Emergency off now offer **Undo** for 5 seconds,
  in the Studio, the Command Center and after a voice command. Undo puts back exactly what was on screen.
- **Smart Next.** Switch it on in Settings → General → Running the meeting, and Next shows the live Frame's
  questions one by one, then its scriptures, and then goes to the next Frame. It works from the Studio, the Command
  Center, the phone and voice commands. Off by default: Next goes to the next Frame, as before.
- **Recovered after a crash.** If FrameDecks closes unexpectedly while a Frame is live, the live Frame is put back as
  soon as it starts again, and the Studio tells you once.
- **Sounds (optional).** A soft chime on Go Live and Next, only on your PC. Off by default (Settings → General).

## Enhancements
- **Presses show at once.** A change is drawn the moment you press, before the PC confirms it; if the PC says no,
  it is put back.
- **Find any setting.** Settings search now looks inside each page, opens the right one and lights the setting up.
- **Reset this section.** Each Settings page with settings of its own has a Reset button (with a confirmation).
  Your meetings, Frames and members are never touched.
- **One motion language and depth.** Every panel, chip and button now moves with the same timing and sits on the
  same three levels of shadow, in dark and light mode.
- **Colour with meaning.** Hide all is a calm button in the Command Center and on the phone; red stays for what is
  on air.
- **Numbers that do not jump.** Timers, counts and times use even-width digits.
- **Calm while live.** While you are live, the Studio's side panel goes quiet in colour (never harder to read) until you reach for it.
- **Nothing blank.** The picture shows a soft shimmer until its first frame arrives.
- **FrameDecks icons.** Go Live, Frame and Deck have their own icons, with a lighter line everywhere.

## Fixes
None in this version.

## Performance Improvements
- Presses no longer wait for the PC to answer before the screen changes.

## Known Issues
