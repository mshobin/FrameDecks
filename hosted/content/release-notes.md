## FrameDecks v26.11.57
One app for the whole Sunday service and for online Bible study: a Song Library, the room screen and a stage screen,
typed answers from members' phones, and a calmer, faster Studio.
Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and
keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Song Library.** Keep every song once and add it to any Frame in two clicks. Paste the words and FrameDecks splits
  them into verses and choruses, or import ChordPro, OpenLyrics or the text of a PowerPoint. Set the arrangement
  (*V1 C V2 C B C*) without copying words, and jump while live with **1–9** for verses and **C**, **B**, **P**, **E**
  for chorus, bridge, pre-chorus and ending. Each song carries its author, copyright and CCLI number, with your CCLI
  licence on screen, and the **usage report** counts each song once per service for your monthly CCLI report.
- **The room screen.** Choose the projector or TV by its name (Settings → How people see it → Projector, TV and OBS).
  **Go Live** can open the Share Window full screen there for you.
- **Stage screen.** A page for musicians and speakers on any screen or tablet: what is on screen now, what comes next,
  the clock, the time left, and a message you type to the stage.
- **Themes.** One background, a picture or a looping video, behind every song, scripture or announcement. Choose it
  once; songs added later get it too.
- **Show a verse.** While live, type a reference in the box above Next (*Rom 8 28*) and press Enter: the verse goes
  straight on screen. A long passage is split into parts that fit, and Next shows the rest.
- **Sunday Service** in Quick Start: a Welcome Loop (countdown and announcements that take turns, then the service
  starts by itself), songs, the reading, the message, the offering and the blessing.
- **Typed answers from members' phones.** Ask everyone to type a short answer (*What stood out to you?*). Answers
  arrive in the Studio without names; **Show** puts the one you choose on screen. The answers you showed go into the
  notes you share with members' phones.
- **Discussion minutes.** Give a question its minutes. When it is shown, the time left counts down above Next and on
  the stage screen, and FrameDecks tells you when the time is up. **Breakout** puts the countdown on screen with the
  question kept visible, for breakout rooms and table groups.
- **A lesson's Reading Frame.** When a lesson has several days, Import a lesson can start with one Frame holding the
  whole week's reading, every passage in order.

## Enhancements
- **Next says where it goes:** *Next ▸ Chorus*, *Next ▸ Question 2*, *Next ▸ Welcome* — for songs, steps, questions
  and the next Frame.
- **Smart Next is on** for new and existing meetings: Next walks the scripture and questions of a Frame, then the next
  Frame. Each Frame has one order, set with **Question · Scripture · Both** or **Set order**.
- **Auto** now steps through a Frame's order on a timer, and can loop it.
- **Run order** opens beside the Frames list as well as on its own (**Open large**).
- **One Import menu** for PowerPoint, a lesson, Excel and .frame files, and one grouped **Add** menu: Worship, Word,
  Church life, Camera and screen.
- **One status pill:** *Ready*, *Live* or *LIVE* — press it to see every output. A separate warning shows only when
  something needs you.
- **One button for each job:** in the Studio the big button in the rail is Go Live and Next, so the top bar no longer
  repeats them. The chips keep their names on wide screens and when you point at them.
- **Videos** can play once and then move on: the time left shows above Next.
- **While live in Simple,** the editor folds away so the stage has the room; open it again whenever you like.
- The Frames panel is tidier: Sync at the top, then **Quick Start**, **Run order** and **+ Frame**.
- Wording: *slides* instead of *deck* where it means a PowerPoint.

## Fixes
- Design in Simple now switches to Advanced instead of doing nothing.
- Slides and pictures count as *on screen* in the status pill.

## Performance Improvements
- The Studio opens with about a fifth of the data it used to download (1.3 MB instead of 6.2 MB) and is ready sooner,
  even on older PCs. Reopening it loads almost nothing.
- Fonts, pictures and scripts are cached and compressed; parts used now and then load the first time you use them.
- Fewer background checks while the Studio sits idle.

## Known Issues
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
