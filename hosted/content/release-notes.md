## FrameDecks v26.11.33

Songs, speaker name tags, polls from members' phones, and a timing plan. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or
let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings,
notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings. Each of the four is a switch in **Settings → Start here → Features**.

## What's New
- **Songs and lyrics.** Add a **Song** Deck, paste a hymn, and it splits into verses and choruses at the blank lines.
  **Next** steps through the verses first, then the Frames, from the Studio, the Command Center, the phone remote and
  voice commands. The title and the verse ("Amazing Grace · Verse 2") show with the words; the credit line shows on the
  first and last verse.
- **Speaker name tags.** **Engage → Name tags**: "John Mathew · Reading" across the bottom of the picture in two clicks.
  Choose a member (their role fills in), type a name, or pick one of the last eight. It stays until you hide it, or for
  8 or 15 seconds, and belongs to the Frame on air.
- **Polls from members' phones.** **Engage → Polls**, while members' phones are open: Yes / No, a poll with 2 to 6
  answers, or a rating from 1 to 5. Phones answer under the screen on the follow page and can change their answer until
  you close it. You choose whether phones see the results, and **Show on screen** puts them on the picture as a Memory
  Card.
- **Timing plan.** Give each Frame its planned minutes and a quiet pill beside Go Live says "On time · 3:10 of 5 min" or
  "2 min over", only to you.

## Enhancements
- The meeting summary counts the polls you asked.
- The Bible study set in Features switches on all four; Simple keeps the timing plan only.

## Known Issues
- Polls need the workspace update in this release (migration 0022) applied to your FrameDecks workspace.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
