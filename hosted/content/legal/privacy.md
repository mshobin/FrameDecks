# FrameDecks Privacy Policy

*Effective 29 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-29*

FrameDecks is made by **Shobin Mathews** ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the personal data described here.
Contact: privacy@framedecks.com.

## The short version

- The FrameDecks program runs on your own PC and works without an account. Pictures, video, the camera and what is on
  screen never leave that PC.
- An account is needed only to plan together on framedecks.com and to sync a PC with a workspace.
- No advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data.

## What we keep, and why

| Data | Why | Kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google or Microsoft | To sign you in and show who did what | While your account exists |
| Your display name, workspaces and role in each | To give you access to the right workspaces | While your account exists |
| When you accepted these terms (version and time) | To show you agreed | While your account exists |
| A workspace's plan: Frame names, words (scripture, questions, scripts) and order, with 30 days of earlier versions | To sync the plan between the PC and the website, and to undo changes | While the workspace exists; earlier versions 30 days |
| A workspace's member list (names and, if the workspace adds them, country, phone, email, birth date, role, groups) | For the workspace's own use: birthdays, invitations, groups | While the workspace keeps them |
| Linked PCs' names, and a log of who changed people, roles, invitations and PCs | Security and accountability | Log: one year |
| Update checks from the program: the program version and Windows version | To offer the right update | Not stored with any identity; Cloudflare keeps request logs briefly |

We rely on your agreement and on what is needed to provide the Service you asked for. A workspace that keeps a member
list is responsible for having permission to keep it.

## What never leaves your PC

Pictures, videos, PDFs, PowerPoint files, audio, the camera picture and what is on screen. Nothing is sent while you
present except commands you choose to send from your phone through the website.

## Who processes it for us

- **Supabase** — database and sign-in, for accounts and workspaces.
- **Cloudflare** — the website, downloads, the update service and email forwarding.
- **Google** and **Microsoft** — only if you choose to sign in with them; they tell us your email address and name,
  never your password.

They act on our instructions and may store data outside your country, protected by their own security and contractual
safeguards.

## Cookies and storage

framedecks.com uses only essential storage in your browser (your sign-in, your theme, your choices). There are no
advertising or tracking cookies. See the Cookie Policy.

## Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your data:

- **Export:** Your account → **Download my data** gives you a file with your account, your workspaces and the workspace
  records you can see.
- **Delete:** Your account → **Delete my account** removes your account, your memberships and invitations at once. A
  workspace you are the only person in is deleted with it. Your name is removed from the change log.
- Workspace owners and admins can remove members and whole workspaces.
- Encrypted nightly backups are kept 30 days, so deleted data leaves the backups within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your
data protection authority. Write to privacy@framedecks.com; we answer within 30 days.

## Children

The Service is not meant for children under 16. A workspace may list young people as members (for example, a youth
group); the workspace is responsible for having permission from their parents or guardians.

## Changes

We will post changes here and, if they matter, tell you when you next sign in.
