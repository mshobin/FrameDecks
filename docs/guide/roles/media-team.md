# Media team

## Your responsibilities

- Turn the leader's plan into Frames and Content.
- Prepare media: pictures, videos, PowerPoint decks, song words.
- Fetch or type every scripture.
- Check everything on the Studio Canvas before the day.
- Keep the PC tidy: files on the PC, backups made.

## Five things to know

1. **+ Frame, then + Deck.** + Frame adds a blank Frame after the selected one; select it, then **+ Deck** to add a Question, Scripture, Text, Image, Video, Timer or PowerPoint. The Frame's Content is listed under **Decks**, below the Frames.
2. **Every Frame owns its Content.** Changing a question in one Frame never changes another. **Duplicate Frame** (right-click, or **Ctrl+D**) makes a fully separate copy right after it.
3. **Content Settings has everything.** One view: Content (its label and words) first, then sections that open with a click: Position and size, Look, Animation, Show on; Media for pictures, video and slides.
4. **FrameDecks keeps copies of your files.** Added pictures, videos and slides keep working if the originals move.
5. **Excel is optional.** The Meeting Workbook builds a whole meeting from a spreadsheet; the app is the source of truth after import.

## Building the meeting

1. Make one Frame per part of the service, in order. Name each one clearly (double-click its name, type, **Enter**): *Welcome*, *Worship 1*, *Reading*, *Discussion*, *Closing*.
2. Add Content to each Frame:
   - **Question**: add questions to its list; double-click a question's label or text to edit it in place.
   - **Scripture**: type a reference and **Fetch scripture** (ESV), or choose **Fetch from → Type it myself**.
   - **Text**: announcements, song words (one Frame per verse or chorus), prayer points.
   - **PowerPoint**: a .pptx or PDF; set **Start at slide** if needed.
   - **Video / Image**: choose the file, then place it.
   - **Timer**: a countdown before the service starts.
3. Arrange the order of questions or scriptures by **dragging** them, or with ↑ / ↓.
4. Check the look in **Position and size** and **Look** (position, width, text size, opacity).
5. Paste the leader's talking points into each Frame's **Teleprompter script** (Content Settings) if they send them. Only the leader sees it, in the Teleprompter window over Zoom or Teams or on the phone remote. Read it once in the Teleprompter (**R**) to check that the lines are not too long for a small window.
6. To change several Frames at once, **Ctrl+Click** or **Shift+Click** them, then copy (**Ctrl+C**), paste (**Ctrl+V**), duplicate (**Ctrl+D**) or delete them. **Ctrl+K** finds any Frame, question or setting.
7. For the Branding badge, choose Logo only / Name only / Logo + Name, a corner, size and plate in its Content Settings. If the logo has a white box around it, use **Remove background** (Settings → Meeting & Branding).

See [Content](../content.md) and [Media](../media.md) for every option.

## Before the service

- [ ] Every Frame checked on the Studio Canvas (select it; the canvas shows a preview without going on air).
- [ ] Every scripture has its text (fetched ones are kept on the PC for offline use).
- [ ] Every PowerPoint opens and its slides look right.
- [ ] Videos play; their sound plan agreed with the sound team (FrameDecks doesn't carry audio).
- [ ] Spelling checked; double-click any card on the canvas to correct it.
- [ ] **Settings → Data / Backup → Back up now**.

## During the service

Usually the service operator runs the controls. Stay available to:
- fix a typo (double-click the card on the canvas, correct, **Enter**);
- add a last-minute Text Content (a prayer request, a notice);
- find a file or slide the leader asks for. **Alt+Space** opens **Spotlight** from anywhere in Windows, even with FrameDecks in the tray; type a few letters of the Frame, question or setting.

> **Important:** Avoid editing the Frame that is live unless you must: changes to it appear on screen as soon as they are saved.

## After the service

- [ ] **Back up now**.
- [ ] Save reusable Frames as templates (**Save as template** in Content Settings → Frame).
- [ ] Export the meeting to Excel if it should be kept (Meeting menu → **Export this meeting to Excel**). To move it to a service PC without internet, use **Settings → Data / Backup → Export Frame File** instead.

## When something goes wrong

| Situation | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A PowerPoint won't convert | Save it as PDF and add the PDF. See [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md#powerpoint-and-media) |
| A scripture won't fetch | Use **Type it myself** and paste the text |
| Wrong content went on screen | Operator: **Hide all** (**Esc**); then fix the Frame |
| An Excel import went wrong | Meeting menu → **Undo the last Excel import** |
