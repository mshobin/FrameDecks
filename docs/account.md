# Your FrameDecks account (optional)

Sign in to keep your Frames the same on every computer you use: the church PC, your laptop at home, a second PC at
the venue. FrameDecks works fully without an account, and presenting never needs the internet.

**Who this is for:** anyone who prepares on one computer and presents on another, or shares preparation with a team.

## What is synced

| Synced | Never synced |
| --- | --- |
| Frames, with their Decks and Content (text, questions, scriptures, settings) | Media files: images, videos, PowerPoint, logos (only their names) |
| Your templates | What is on screen, and the cards' text at that moment |
| Saved questions and scriptures | The FrameCast camera's settings, recordings |
| Branding, layout, card looks, the meeting profile | Your members list, birthdays and email list |
| Meeting details (presenter, contact, attendees, notes): **only if you turn them on** | The phone remote's PIN |

A Frame that uses an image or video shows it on another computer once that file is on that computer too.

## Sign in

1. Open **Settings → FrameDecks account**.
2. Either:
   - type your email, select **Email me a code**, and enter the 6-digit code from the email (it works for 10
     minutes); or
   - select **Google, Microsoft or Apple…**: your browser opens; sign in there, then come back to FrameDecks.
3. The first time you sign in, an account is made for you, with a workspace for your ministry.

On a computer that already has Frames, they are added to the account. If the account already has Frames from
another computer, both sets are kept.

## How sync works

- Every change is saved on this computer first, exactly as before. Sync sends it a few seconds later, and checks for
  changes from your other computers every minute (and when you select **Sync now**).
- If the internet is down, nothing about presenting changes. Sync catches up when the connection is back.
- Going live, stepping through Frames and Decks, hiding and showing Content are never sent, so presenting on one
  computer never changes what another one shows.

## When the same Frame changed on two computers

No Frame is ever lost. If you changed a Frame on two computers before they synced, the account keeps the version that
synced first, and the other one is added as a copy named "‹Frame› (from ‹computer›)". Keep the one you want and
delete the other.

For settings (branding, layout and the others), the latest change wins.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sync meeting details** | Off unless you turn it on. Sends the presenter, contact, attendees and notes from the Meeting Form. |
| **Pause sync while something is on screen** | Holds sync during a service; it catches up afterwards. |

## Sign out, export and delete

- **Sign out of this computer** keeps every Frame on this computer and stops syncing it.
- Your data in the account can be downloaded or deleted at any time (export and delete come to Settings with the
  next update; until then, ask and it is done for you).
