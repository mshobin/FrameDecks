# Service-day checklist

A printable timeline for running a service with FRAMEDECKS, plus an emergency card to keep beside the PC.

**Who this is for:** service operators, remote operators and leaders. Print it, or keep it open on a second screen.

## The day before

- [ ] The meeting is complete: every Frame in order, with its Content.
- [ ] The leader has reviewed it and written the Teleprompter script for each Frame.
- [ ] Every scripture has its text; every PowerPoint and video opens.
- [ ] Song words and lyrics licences checked.
- [ ] **Settings → Data / Backup → Back up now**.

## 60 minutes before

- [ ] PC on mains power; Windows updates paused; notifications off.
- [ ] FRAMEDECKS open; **FRAMEDECKS** camera ready in the command bar status.
- [ ] Webcam, microphone and projector connected.
- [ ] Meeting app (Zoom / Teams / Meet) or OBS open, with **FRAMEDECKS** as the camera.
- [ ] Projector: **Share Window** → Presentation window on the projector → full screen.

## 30 minutes before

- [ ] Phone remote on; remote operator connected (**Connected** shown).
- [ ] Walk through the first Frames with **Go Live**; check the projector and the meeting app's self-view.
- [ ] **Teleprompter** open where the leader can see it (docked in the Studio, the **Teleprompter window** on a second screen, or on the leader's phone).
- [ ] Leader on Zoom or Teams from the same PC: **Teleprompter overlay** open (**Shift+R**). Check that it sits over the participants (not over the leader's own video or the chat they need), that it is in the right mode (usually **Meeting**, key **3**), and that **Stay on top** (📌) is on: click into Zoom and the overlay should stay in front.
- [ ] Spotlight works: press **Alt+Space** (or the shortcut shown in **Settings → General → Window and tray**) with Zoom in front.
- [ ] Agree who controls Next (PC or phone) and the hand signals.

## 10 minutes before

- [ ] Opening Frame live (for example, a countdown Timer or Welcome).
- [ ] Blackout, Freeze and Annotate all **off** (the Program button reads **Program ▾**).
- [ ] Phones on Do Not Disturb; screens set to stay awake.

## During the service

- [ ] Follow the leader's pace.
- [ ] **Freeze** before changing files or windows on the PC.
- [ ] Keep this checklist's [emergency card](#emergency-card) in view.

## After the service

- [ ] **Hide all**; end the meeting or stream.
- [ ] **Back up now**.
- [ ] Save reusable Frames as templates; export the meeting to Excel if it should be kept.
- [ ] Write down what to improve next week.

---

## Emergency card

> Cut along the line and keep it beside the PC.

| If… | Press | On the phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anything wrong is on screen | **Esc** (Hide all) | **HIDE ALL** |
| The screen must go dark | **K** (Blackout) | **BLACK SCREEN** |
| You need time to fix something | **F** (Freeze) | **FREEZE** |
| You need a message on screen | **Program → Emergency** | Advanced remote → **Emergency** |
| Ready again | Select the Frame → **T** (Go Live) | **NEXT FRAME** / **PREVIOUS FRAME** |

**Nothing above deletes anything.** When the problem is solved, put the right Frame back with Go Live.

Camera missing in Zoom? Choose **FRAMEDECKS** again, or restart Zoom.
Phone disconnected? Carry on from the PC.
