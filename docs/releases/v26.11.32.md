## FrameDecks v26.11.32

Settings, in six groups you can name. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It
installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and
recordings. No setting changes: each one simply lives where you would look for it.

## What's New
- **Settings in six groups.** **Start here** (Features, Running the meeting) · **Meeting look** · **Content**
  (Scripture, Birthdays, Memory Cards) · **How people see it** (FrameDecks vCam and Projector, TV and OBS as its own
  pages, and Listening) · **People & devices** (Phone & iPad Remote, Sync, Security) · **This PC** (Window &
  performance, Keyboard shortcuts, Data / Backup, Diagnostics, About).
- **Listening, in one place.** Listen for scripture, Voice commands and Follow my voice: everything that can hear you,
  on one page.
- **Memory Cards in Settings.** Where the bubbles go, how long they stay, and whether they show on the Share Window,
  the same choices as the gear on the Memory Cards pane.

## Enhancements
- Recording is on the FrameDecks vCam page: it records exactly what the camera sends.
- Features has its own page, first in Settings.
- FrameDecks vCam and Projector, TV and OBS show as pages of How people see it.
