## FrameDecks v26.11.26

Getting into your workspace takes no typing now: scan a code, press one button, or touch Face ID or Windows Hello.
Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and
keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Add a device opens your workspace as you.** In FrameDecks, Settings → Sync → **Add a device** shows a code. Scan
  it with your phone's camera and your workspace opens on the phone, as you: nothing to type and nobody to approve.
  The same works from **Devices** on the website. The code works once, for 2 minutes.
- **Connect this PC from the website.** Signed in on framedecks.com, choose **Connect this PC** on Home. FrameDecks
  opens on this PC and asks once which team it is joining: choose **Connect**. No code to type.
- **One touch next time.** After a device is trusted, the website offers to save a passkey: from then on Face ID, a
  fingerprint or Windows Hello opens your workspace.
- **Continue with Google, Apple or Microsoft** on the website, for someone new.

## Known Issues
- Passkeys and Google, Apple or Microsoft show only once the workspace owner has switched them on.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
