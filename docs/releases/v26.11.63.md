## FrameDecks v26.11.63
Meeting together: hands up for sharing, a Bible quiz on phones, a take-home page for the week, the prayer journey,
care and attendance, chords for musicians, a co-leader seat and a health check after every meeting.
Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and
keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Chat Bubbles and recordings.

## What's New
- **Discussion helper.** With members' phones open, switch on **I'd like to share**: people raise a hand on their
  phone, and you see the queue in the order hands went up. Press **Shared** to lower a hand and tick that person off;
  **Not shared yet** lists everyone who gave a first name and has not spoken. A quiet timer for each question shows
  only on your PC. First names are always optional.
- **Bible quiz.** FrameDecks makes questions from the meeting's own scriptures (fill the gap, or "Where is this
  written?"). Change any of them or write your own, then **Ask**: everyone answers on their phone with a countdown,
  the question appears on screen as a Chat Bubble, and **Show the answer** marks the right one. Quick right answers
  score more; put the **Leaderboard** on screen whenever you like.
- **Take-home page.** After the meeting, give members a page to keep for the week on the same follow page: the
  scriptures, the questions to reflect on, the songs, the sermon notes if you shared them, prayer requests you marked
  OK to share, answered prayers, and when you meet next with what to read. Add a note of your own. It stays up to two
  weeks, then is removed by itself.
- **Prayer journey.** Every prayer request from members' phones is now kept on your PC, encrypted to your Windows
  account. Mark each one **Still praying** or **Answered** (with how it was answered). Requests are private unless you
  tick **OK to share**, and shared ones never show a name. Put **Answered prayers** on screen as a Chat Bubble. The
  person who asked can see on their own phone whether their request is still being prayed for or answered.
- **Care and attendance.** When members' phones close, the first names people typed are kept on your PC with the
  date. **Care and attendance** shows who is new, who has missed the last two meetings, and how many joined each week,
  so you know who to reach out to. Remove any meeting or name at any time.
- **Chords for musicians.** Songs keep their [G] chords. While a Song is on screen, musicians on members' phones get
  a **Chords** tab with the words and chords, following along, with **Transpose** and **Capo**. Switch it off in the
  Song Library (**Chords on musicians' phones**).
- **Co-leader seat.** Hand the meeting to a co-leader for a while: **Co-leader** makes a second PIN, just for them.
  Their phone opens the phone remote with a focused layout (Next, Previous, Hide all, the Content and Chat Bubbles).
  The Studio shows **Co-leader connected**, and **Take back** ends it at once.
- **Meeting health check.** The meeting summary now opens with how the timing went against your plan: which Frames
  ran over, which were quicker, what was not reached, and a few tips for next week. Only you see it; it is left out
  of Print, Copy and Email.

## Enhancements
- **FrameDecks is open source again**, under the Apache License 2.0: anyone may read, use, change and share it. The
  program from framedecks.com still comes with the End User License Agreement, which now covers the official program
  and the FrameDecks name and emblem. The About screen and the website say so.
- The About screen and the phone remote's credit line give hello@framedecks.com as the contact address.
- Members' phones has quick links to Discussion, Bible quiz, Take-home page and Prayer journey under the code.
- The meeting summary has a **Take-home page** button.
- Closing members' phones keeps prayer requests in the Prayer journey and keeps today's first names for Care and
  attendance; the confirmation says so.
- Discussion, Bible quiz and Co-leader are in the Command Center, the top bar chips and Search (Ctrl+K), with Take-home
  page, Prayer journey and Care and attendance.

## Known Issues
- If you lower someone's hand while their phone is asleep, their phone shows the hand lowered when they next look.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Check the download's SHA-256 on the download page first. Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is
  published.
