## FrameDecks v26.11.4

Help, right where you need it. When the phone won't connect, FrameDecks now checks every step and fixes the first
problem in one click; every problem has a short code you can look up; a small ? opens the right page of the guide;
and when you still need us, one button sends a report you can read first. Download from **framedecks.com/download**,
or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members,
settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Check my connection** (Settings → Phone & iPad Remote): every step from the PC to the phone, in order, each with
  a tick. The first problem shows what happened and one button that fixes it: turn the remote on, restart it, open
  Wi-Fi or network settings, or allow FrameDecks through Windows Firewall (Windows asks first). The last step turns
  green the moment your phone arrives.
- **Error codes**: every connection problem has a short code (like FD-NET-02), the same on the PC, the phone and the
  website, with what happened, why and the fix. framedecks.com/help → Error codes lists them all.
- **Send a report to support** (Settings → Diagnostics, or straight from a failed connection check): the whole report
  on screen first, with a reference number, then Email support, Copy or Save. No PIN, keys, file paths, plan or
  member details, and nothing is sent until you choose.
- **Every key on one page**: press ? in the Command Center (Alt+Space) to see all its keys. Settings → Keyboard
  shortcuts now lists them too, above the Studio's own keys.
- **What's new**: after an update, one small card shows the three changes worth knowing, each with Show me.

## Enhancements
- A small **?** next to the phone remote, FrameDecks vCam, Capture Memory, Workspace Sync and Follow my voice opens
  the right page of the guide.
- The phone remote has **Test connection** (how quickly the PC answers) and a **Help** link.
- On framedecks.com: **search** across the whole Help Center, **Ask Help** (choose a problem and go through it one
  step at a time, or type a question or an error code; nothing you type leaves the page), short **clips** of Go Live,
  Spotlight, Memory Cards, Check my connection and the phone remote, and **Was this helpful?** under every page.
- Linking a PC on the website now waits for the PC to confirm before it says done.

## Fixes
None in this version.

## Performance Improvements
- The Help clips load only when scrolled near, and show a still image to anyone who asked for less motion.

## Known Issues
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
