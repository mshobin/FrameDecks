## FrameDecks v26.11.37

One simple way into your workspace: your phone is the key. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let
FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings,
notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Open in this PC's browser.** Settings → Sync has a new button that opens your workspace on framedecks.com in this
  PC's own browser, already as you: no code to scan, no passkey, no email.
- **Your phone is the key.** On framedecks.com, **Open with your phone** is the main way in: this browser shows a
  code, you scan it with a phone that is already in your team, allow it, and the workspace opens.
- **A flowchart you can follow.** Opening your workspace on framedecks.com is drawn as a short checklist: each step
  ticks in turn, a line runs between them, and the last one connects into a picture of your workspace, which lights
  up with its name when it opens. The page you reach from a scanned code works the same way.
- **Keep your workspace safe.** After a phone joins by scanning, it asks once to add an email (for a lost phone) and
  to save a passkey for one-touch opening next time. **Not now** is remembered.

## Enhancements
- The passkey button shows only on a browser that has saved a FrameDecks passkey, so there is no empty "Choose a
  passkey" box elsewhere. Other browsers find it under **Other ways in**.
- The phone and website stop offering **Connect this PC** once a PC is connected to the team, and say which PC it is.

## Fixes
- A phone added from the program no longer seemed to need a passkey it never had.

## Performance Improvements
- The website's JavaScript is a little smaller (two styling helpers replaced by a few lines of our own).

## Known Issues
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
