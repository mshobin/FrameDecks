## FrameDecks v26.11.16

The meeting's controls wherever you are: Emergency screen and the meeting clock on the Command Center and your
phone, Mute and Camera from the phone, and verses from your plan shown at once. Download from
**framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all
your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Emergency screen on the Command Center and the phone.** In **Control**, beside Black screen and Freeze. Press it
  twice to put your message on screen, and once to take it off. With no message set it says "We'll be right back."
- **The meeting clock in Control.** The Command Center and the phone show the time since Go Live and the live Frame's
  time against its planned time, in gold once it runs over, as in the Studio's Frames list.
- **Mute and Camera from the phone.** **Control → Meeting app** presses Mute or Camera in Zoom, Teams or Google Meet
  on the PC. Leave and Share stay on the PC, so a phone can never end your meeting.
- **Listen for scripture: verses from your plan.** Say a verse that is already in this meeting's plan, and the planned
  passage is offered, marked **in your plan**. For example, say "Romans eight thirty-eight" and Romans 8:38–39 is
  offered. It is shown from the plan itself: instantly, and also offline.
- **Listen for scripture: Earlier.** The last three verses heard stay one tap away on the Command Center, the phone
  and in the Studio, so a verse missed in the moment can still be shown.

## Enhancements
- **The Teleprompter is in Engage only**, on the Command Center, the top bar and the phone, because it is used live.
- **The Command Center's Play button** shows only when the Frame has a video, which leaves room for Emergency.

## Fixes
None in this version.

## Performance Improvements
- The clock on the Command Center and the phone ticks only while a Frame is live and the screen is in view.

## Known Issues
- Mute and Camera from the phone, and Listen for scripture, need the FrameDecks app on Windows. Listen for scripture
  hears this PC's microphone, not the people in Zoom or Teams.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
