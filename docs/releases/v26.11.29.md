## FrameDecks v26.11.29

One button for going live and going off air, without the risk of an accidental off. Download from
**framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your
meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Off air, by holding Go Live.** When the Frame you have selected is the one on air, the top bar's **Go Live** reads
  **Off air**, in calm colours. Press and hold it for a second (a thin bar fills along the button) to take everything
  off screen; **Undo** is there for 5 seconds. A quick press, or letting go sooner, never goes off air. Select any other
  Frame and the button is Go Live again. The keyboard can hold it too: Space or Enter.
- After the last Frame, the Live Stage's button (beside the picture) turns into **Off air** the same way, so you can
  end the meeting from there.
