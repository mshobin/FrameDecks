## FrameDecks v26.11.12

A calmer Studio that is simple by default, a bigger live picture, and a new way to add phones and computers to
your team: scan a code, with no passwords. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update
itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory
Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Scan to join.** To add a phone, tablet or computer to your team, choose **Settings → Sync → Add a device** on
  the church PC. A QR code appears. Scan it with the phone's camera, choose **Join**, and you're in. You don't need an
  email or a password. A computer without a camera can type the short code printed under the QR code instead. A work
  computer can also show its own code (framedecks.com → **Open Workspace** → **Show a code for this browser**). A
  phone that is already in your team, or the church PC (**Allow a device**), then lets it in.
- **Invite someone** as a Helper (plans and runs services) or an Admin (also adds and removes devices). Codes work
  once and expire after 2 minutes.
- **One Devices list** shows every phone, computer and church PC in your team, in FrameDecks and on framedecks.com.
  **Remove** takes a device out straight away.
- **Recovery.** The owner can print a recovery code on framedecks.com (**Devices**). It gets the team back if every
  device is lost. The owner can add an emergency email there too.
- **How people see it: Camera, Share a window or Both.** You choose once, in the Welcome tour or in **Settings → How
  people see it**. With **Share a window**, FrameDecks works without the virtual camera, and the camera stays
  off and uses no power. **Use the camera too** switches it back on.
- **Simple and Advanced.** The Studio now opens in **Simple**, which shows only what you need to run a meeting.
  **Advanced** (above the Studio Canvas) shows every control again. Nothing has been removed or renamed, and
  FrameDecks remembers your choice on this PC.

## Enhancements
- **The live picture is bigger.** The Studio Canvas is a centred 16:9 picture, about a fifth wider on a laptop
  screen. A small **Live output** panel beside it shows only what FrameDecks really knows: the camera and its frame
  rate, the phone remote, what is on screen, and any warning. In Simple it says it all in one sentence, such as
  *All good. People see "Welcome".* or *Ready. Press Go Live when the meeting starts.* Click the sentence for the
  details.
- **One clear signal when you're live.** In Simple, a green ring goes round the picture while it is live. The
  extra badges step back.
- **A calmer top bar.** Simple shows Previous, Go Live, Next, the item arrows, the chips (with their names) and
  Hide all. Transition and Auto-advance are in Advanced.
- **The editor shows your words first.** In Simple, Position and size, Look, Animation and Show on sit behind one
  **Design** button.
- **The same words everywhere.** The Command Center and the phone remote say **Go Live** and **Black screen**, like
  the Studio.
- **Sync is simpler.** **Settings → Sync** is one page: Add a device, Invite someone, Allow a device, Devices, and
  one status line. **Sync now** replaces Resync Linked PCs. On framedecks.com, Service PCs and Manage Devices are now
  one **Devices** page.
- **Up next is a slimmer strip** under the picture, so the editor has more room.

## Fixes
- On a phone, the Join page now shows the form right under the logo, so you don't have to scroll after scanning.

## Performance Improvements
- With **Share a window**, the virtual camera does not start, which saves processor time on slower PCs.

## Known Issues
- Scan to join needs the website update that comes with this version. Until it is live, **Add a device** shows a
  code that the website does not accept yet. You can still use email to open your workspace.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
