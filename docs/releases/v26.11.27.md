## FrameDecks v26.11.27

Run more of the meeting with your voice, without surprises. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let
FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings, notes,
pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Say a Frame's name.** Hold **Right Ctrl** and say "go to Welcome" or "open Bible Study", then let go. The name of a
  Scripture or Question Deck in the live Frame works too ("Discussion"). Renamed or new Frames are understood at once,
  and **Undo** is there for 5 seconds.
- **A verse asks first.** Hold Right Ctrl and say "show John three sixteen": it comes up in the Studio and in the
  corner card as **You asked for John 3:16**, with **Show**. Press it, or keep holding and say "show it". Nothing goes
  on screen by itself, even with Auto on.
- Going Live, leaving the call, sharing your screen, ending the meeting and deleting stay a press: never by voice.

## Enhancements
- The website's features heading now reads **Prepared before. Confident during.**
- The front page's stage moves one chapter per scroll, swipe or wheel turn, and each chapter then plays by itself.

## Known Issues
- Voice commands need Settings → Scripture → Voice commands switched on, and work while a Frame is live.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
