## FrameDecks v26.11.38

Open your workspace from any computer with a code from your email. Download from **framedecks.com/download**, or let
FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all your meetings, members, settings,
notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **Email me a code.** On framedecks.com, Open your workspace now starts with your email. Type it, choose **Email me a
  code**, type the 6-digit code from the email, and your workspace opens. The flowchart ticks as you go: *Code sent →
  Code entered → your workspace opens*.
- **Only the code, no link.** The email shows the code large and nothing to click, so work email that opens links by
  itself can't use it up. The code works once, for 10 minutes.
- **Whose computer is this?** Choose **Mine** to stay open on that computer, or **Someone else's** for a work or
  borrowed computer: your workspace closes when you close the tab. A computer that never opened FrameDecks starts on
  Someone else's.
- **You hear where it was opened.** After a code opens your workspace you get an email: "BSF Study was opened on Chrome
  on Windows", with the time. Not you, or left open at work? Devices has a new **Sign out other computers**.

## Enhancements
- Six boxes for the code: paste it, let your phone fill it in from the email, or type it; the last digit opens.
  **Send a new code** unlocks after 30 seconds.
- An email with no workspace is answered just like a sent code and never starts an empty workspace; only **Start with
  email** makes a new one.
- **Open with your phone** and the passkey are still there, under Other ways in.
- The Help guide's Open Workspace steps and picture show the new screen.

## Fixes
- The sign-in email now carries the 6-digit code the website asks for (it sent only a link before).

## Performance Improvements
None in this version.

## Known Issues
- The email telling you where your workspace was opened needs the workspace update in this release (migration 0024).
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
