## FrameDecks v26.11.36

Your workspace without Google or Microsoft, and a first sync that adds things smartly. Download from
**framedecks.com/download**, or let FrameDecks update itself. It installs over every earlier version and keeps all
your meetings, members, settings, notes, pinned Memory Cards and recordings.

## What's New
- **A first sync you see before it happens.** When a PC is connected, nothing changes until you have seen, meeting
  by meeting, what syncing will do: *already in the workspace* (skipped), *only on this PC* (goes up as its own
  meeting), *only in the workspace* (comes to this PC), or *edited in both places*. Then press **Sync now**.
- **Meetings are never mixed.** A meeting is matched by its name (and date); this PC's meetings stay their own instead
  of falling into the workspace's first meeting. When this PC only adds Frames to a meeting, they join it. When a
  meeting was edited in both places you choose: **Keep both** (recommended: "Sunday Service (this PC)"), **Use the
  workspace's** or **Use this PC's**.
- **What already exists is skipped.** Every Frame is compared by its content first, so nothing is copied twice;
  members (by email or phone), verses and questions already in the workspace are not added again, and a member's
  missing details are filled in.
- **Undo this connection.** A copy of this PC's plan is kept before the first sync. For 7 days, Settings → Sync →
  More → Undo this connection takes out what the first sync added, puts back what it changed, and restores this PC.
- **Passkey first, no Google or Microsoft.** framedecks.com opens with a passkey (Face ID, a fingerprint, Windows
  Hello) or a one-time email code. The Google, Apple and Microsoft buttons are gone.
- **New-device alerts.** When a PC is connected or a device joins your team, owners and admins get an email, and
  Devices on framedecks.com shows it at the top.

## Enhancements
- Settings → Sync says "Waiting for your choice" while the first sync waits, and the main window points to it once.

## Fixes
- The first sync no longer moved a PC's Frames into the workspace's first meeting.

## Performance Improvements
None in this version.

## Known Issues
- New-device emails need the workspace update in this release (migration 0023) and the alert function set up.
- The installer is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm: **More info → Run anyway**.
  Each release is checked with Microsoft Defender before it is published.
