# FrameDecks Cookie Policy

*Effective 18 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-18 (all storage listed)*

framedecks.com does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It stores only what it needs to work, in your
browser's storage (local storage, or this tab's session storage until you trust the device):

| Name | What it is for | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sb-…-auth-token` | Keeps your workspace open: in this tab only, or on this device once you choose Trust This Device | This tab, or until you disconnect or remove the device |
| `sb-…-auth-token-code-verifier` | Checks that the one-time link is opened by the browser that asked for it | Until the link is used |
| `fd-trusted-device` | A Trusted Device's token (the cloud keeps only its hash) | Until you remove the device, at most 180 days |
| `fd-trust-decided`, `fd-trust-asked` | Whether you have chosen Trust This Device or Just this visit | Until you change it (this tab for the second) |
| `fd-theme`, `fd-effects` | Light or dark, and Reduce effects | Until you change them |
| `framedecks-workspace`, `fd-guide`, `fd-pc-card-hidden` | The workspace you chose last, the Setup guide's progress, a hidden card | Until you change them |
| `fd-after-sign-in` | Brings you back to a page (such as allowing a PC) after the one-time link | 15 minutes |
| `fd-terms-accepted` | That you ticked the terms on the sign-up form, until your workspace records it | Read once after you open your workspace, at most a day |
| `fd-seen`, `fd-passkey`, `fd-email-later` | That this browser opened a workspace before, and whether you saved a passkey or chose later | Until you change them |
| `fd-plan-changes-…` | Plan changes made on this device that wait for Sync Now | Until they are synced or discarded |
| `fd-voter` | On members' phones: a random number so each phone answers a poll or a question once (the website keeps only its hash) | Until you clear this site's data |
| `fd-voted:<article>` | That you rated a Help article, so it does not ask again | Until you clear this site's data |
| `fd-meeting-<workspace>` | Which meeting you were planning in that workspace, so the page opens on it again | Until you change it |
| `fd-follow-name`, `fd-my-prayers`, `fd-chords` | On members' phones: the first name you typed (optional), the prayer requests this phone sent (to show whether each is answered), and your Transpose and Capo choice | Until you clear this site's data; prayer requests at most the last 30 |
| `framedecks-media` (IndexedDB) | Pictures you add to a plan in this browser, until they go into a .frame file | Until you remove them |
| `fd-consent` | That you have seen this notice | One year |

Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie (such as `__cf_bm`) to protect the site from abuse.

These are strictly necessary, so they do not need your consent; the notice on the site tells you about them. If we ever
add anything that is not strictly necessary, we will ask first and you can say no.

To remove them, choose Disconnect this device and clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Questions: privacy@framedecks.com.
