                          Meeting & Branding

                          Your meeting’s name, logo and colour. They appear only where you choose: Branding Content, and text Content that ask for them.

                          Look

                          The colours of FrameDecks on this PC: the same looks as on framedecks.com.

                          Light or dark

                          Only this window changes. Your camera picture, overlays and the phone remote keep their own look.

                          Features

                          Switch on what you use. What is off leaves every screen, and its work stops.

                          Running the meeting

                          How Next moves, the corner card, sounds and the summary after the meeting.

                          Window & performance

                          The window, the tray, the Command Center and how FrameDecks uses this PC.

                          Memory Cards

                          Copied chat messages shown as bubbles. The same choices as the gear on the Memory Cards pane.

                          Listening

                          Everything that can hear you: a spoken verse, voice commands, and the Teleprompter following your voice. Only on this PC; nothing you say is recorded or sent anywhere.

                          Teleprompter: Follow my voice

                          The script moves as you speak. Its microphone and sensitivity are in the Teleprompter’s top bar, beside Follow my voice, so you can set them while you read.

                          Scripture on a slide

                          Nothing is shown until you press Show, as with a spoken verse.

                          Birthdays

                          Birthday reminders and how dates are read. Placement and style are in the Birthday Content’s settings.

                          Birthdays

                          The greeting, card size, position and transition are on the Birthday Content (select it in a Frame).

                          Reminders and dates

                          Remind me
                          29 February
                          Dates like 03/04/1990

                          Only used when importing a date that could be read either way. Dates in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 1985-03-15) are never ambiguous.

                          Scripture

                          Fetch Bible passages for Scripture Content. Fetched passages are kept on this PC, so a Bible study keeps working without internet.

                          Transitions

                          The transition every Frame uses unless it has its own. The Transition menu in the top bar can override it for one take.

                          How people see it

                          Choose how your meeting reaches people. Most churches use one way; you can use more than one.

                          Projector, TV and OBS

                          Add the overlays to OBS, Camo or Zoom.

                          Set up OBS, Camo or Zoom

                          1. OBS: Sources → + → Browser. Paste a link from “Overlay links” below. Width 1920, height 1080. Keep the source above your camera.
                          2. Zoom: in OBS select Start Virtual Camera, then in Zoom choose OBS Virtual Camera as your camera.
                          3. Camo Studio: add a Web layer with the same link.
                          4. The background around the cards is transparent automatically. “Connected” below shows when a source has an overlay open.

                          Overlay links

                          FrameDecks vCam

                          FrameDecks’s own camera for Zoom and Teams, with optional background blur. No OBS or Camo needed.

                          Recording

                          Record exactly what FrameDecks vCam sends to Zoom or Teams. Recordings are saved on this PC.

                          Phone & iPad Remote

                          Control the overlays from an iPhone, Android phone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi.

                          phone remote

                          Notes on the phone

                          Anyone who has the PIN can open the phone remote, so reading stays off unless you turn it on. Read notes are never kept on the phone: they hide after a minute without use and when the phone sleeps. Notes are never sent to the website.

                          Keyboard shortcuts

                          Every key: anywhere, in the Command Center, and in the Studio (not while typing in a box).

                          Anywhere and in the Command Center

                          Alt+Space works over any app. Press ? in the Command Center to see these there.

                          In the Studio (you can change these)

                          Sync

                          Add your phone and your team’s devices. Everything then stays in step by itself. Optional: services always run from this PC, online or not.

                          This PC and your team

                          Checking…

                          What is synced

                          Synced: Frames and their Content, the order of Frames, questions and scriptures, Teleprompter scripts, card looks, Meeting & Branding names and colour, the Meeting Form, and the member list.

                          Never leaves this PC: pictures, videos, PDFs and PowerPoints (only their file names), the logo picture, what is live on screen, the phone remote PIN, and your private settings.

                          A change to the Frame that is live waits until it leaves the screen. If the same Frame was changed on two computers, both versions are kept.

                          How FrameDecks protects your meeting and your members’ details.

                          Data / Backup

                          Where everything is stored, backups and resets.

                          Verses, questions & overlay settings

                          Saved automatically in kc-saved-content.json in the FrameDecks folder.

                          Everything is saved automatically on this computer. Backup files let you move your lists to another computer.

                          Meeting from Excel

                          Plan meetings on the FrameDecks website’s Meeting Form: it reaches this PC by itself once the PC is connected (Settings → Sync). The Excel Meeting Workbook still works here.

                          Member data & privacy

                          Member information stays on this computer. It is never uploaded, and it is never sent to the overlay pages. The birthday overlay only receives the greeting, the name, and the country if you choose to show it.

                          Stored in: …

                          To restore a backup, use Members → People → Import / Export → Import from Excel or CSV.
